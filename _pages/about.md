I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [School of Computing](https://www.cse.uconn.edu/) at the [University of Connecticut](https://www.uconn.edu), where I work as a Research Assistant.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on the intersection of AI and networking, spanning both **AI for networks** and **networks for AI**. I explore how machine learning and large language models can enable intelligent, automated networked systems, with an emphasis on network design, management, and optimization. I also investigate how network architectures and communication strategies can improve the performance, efficiency, and scalability of distributed AI systems.

Currently, I am exploring the integration of LLMs with networking, focusing on programmable data planes and the P4 language.

Background
======
Before starting my Ph.D., I spent several years in industry building blockchain systems: as a Backend Engineer at [Tencent](https://www.tencent.com) (2022–2025), working on consensus algorithms, smart contracts, and Web3.0 applications; and as a Blockchain Engineer at Jinninghui Technology (2018–2022), where I built a blockchain system and developer platform from the ground up. I received my B.E. from the School of Electronics and Information Technology at Sun Yat-sen University in 2018.
